# Changelog

## 0.1.0

- Torso overlay with a GM-only core slot and three craftable nodes, drawn over the concept sheet.
- Rune library: all Uncommon, Rare and Very rare runes from the tables, with a GM editor for custom runes,
  overrides and disabling.
- Crafting bench: one project at a time, once-per-day work, DMG day counts per rarity, progress ring and bar.
- Disenchanting: break a magic item into an essence that keeps the original data; slot essences into nodes.
- Drag and drop onto slots: items (GM) on the core, essences and magic items on nodes, runes from the picker.
- Day tracking by world time, long rest, or GM fiat; GM shortcuts to add days or finish.
- Layout editor for the image crop and slot positions.
